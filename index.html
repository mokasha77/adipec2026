MOSAM: a sim-to-real tested LiDAR drone twin

Mohamed Sameh (MSc student) · Dr. Haitham Elshimy (supervisor) · Dr. Mohamed Okasha (PI) · Dr. Abdullah Hayat (co-PI)

A multi-sensor X500 quadrotor logs every LiDAR and inertial stream through ROS 2. Its test room is rebuilt in Gazebo with a calibrated LiDAR and IMU model. The model, metrics and pass rules were frozen before new recordings were made, so the result could not be tuned afterwards.

0.10 m

Next: matched real and simulated flights with independent ground truth, then a tank or vessel mock-up trial with an energy-sector partner. Target applications are not yet field-tested.

Graduation project

A flight-calibrated digital twin of the PX4 X500

Al Reem Alshamsi, Alia Alblooshi, Najla Aljasmi, Alyazia Alharthi, Hamda Alhosani · supervisor Dr. Mohamed Okasha

The team rebuilt an X500 with carbon-fibre arms, measured its mass properties and motor thrust, and wrote a 6-DOF MATLAB/Simulink model that the real PX4 flight software flies, with Gazebo as the display.
